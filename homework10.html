Discussion and Conclusions

The survey results show that online shopping has become a common activity among internet users. Most participants reported making purchases online, while only a small number said they browse products without buying.

The 18–25 age group represented the largest portion of online shoppers. Monthly shopping was the most common habit among participants. Amazon and Trendyol were identified as the most popular shopping websites.

Research from Pew Research Center and eMarketer shows that online shopping continues to grow worldwide. Increased internet access, mobile shopping, and secure payment systems contribute to this growth. The survey findings are consistent with these trends.
