Analysis and Conclusions

The survey shows that online shopping is very common among participants. 90% of respondents have purchased products online at least once. Most participants shop online monthly, while a smaller group shops weekly.

Young adults (18–25) represent the largest group of online shoppers. Amazon and Trendyol were the most preferred shopping websites.
