Discussion and Conclusions

The survey results indicate that online shopping has become a common activity among internet users. Recent studies show that the use of artificial intelligence in e-commerce is increasing, with many digital shoppers using AI assistants to help search for products, compare prices, and make purchasing decisions. At the same time, security remains a concern, as approximately one-third of Americans report experiencing an online shopping scam. Research also reveals that mobile commerce accounts for a large share of e-commerce traffic, but it has the lowest conversion rate and a high shopping cart abandonment rate. These findings suggest that while consumers are increasingly comfortable with online shopping and new technologies, factors such as trust, security, and user-friendly payment systems continue to play a major role in purchasing behavior. Overall, the results of this survey are consistent with current e-commerce trends and demonstrate the growing importance of digital shopping in everyday life.
