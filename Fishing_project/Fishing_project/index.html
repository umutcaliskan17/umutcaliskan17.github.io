What should be in your bag?

  • Spare line, hooks and sinkers
  • The right bait or artificial lure
  • Pliers, scissors, bucket and measuring ruler
Tip

For beginners

A simple float rig or a light spinning setup is easier to understand for your first trips. Observing depth, current and shoreline structure is often more important than buying expensive gear.
