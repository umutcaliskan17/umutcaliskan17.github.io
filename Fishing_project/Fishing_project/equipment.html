Equipment

Basic tackle bag

Gear changes depending on the fish you target, but a balanced, simple and durable setup is enough for beginners.

Tackle Bag

Keep small gear organized and ready.

A good tackle bag keeps hooks, sinkers, line, tools and lures separated so you can change rigs quickly without searching through loose gear.

  • Use small boxes for hooks, swivels and sinkers.
  • Keep pliers, scissors and measuring tools easy to reach.
  • Pack only what fits your fishing spot and target species.
