Gear & Tackle

The Ultimate Fishing Equipment Guide

Choosing the right tackle is half the battle in fishing. Explore the specialized rods, reels, and lures tailored for different fishing disciplines below.

1. Fishing Rods & Disciplines

Spin (Cast & Retrieve) Rods

Action & Specs: Typically ranges between 2.40m to 2.70m in length, featuring a casting weight (lure weight) of 10-40g.

Primary Purpose: Designed for casting hard lures and retrieving them continuously to mimic wounded baitfish. It is a highly dynamic and active discipline used to target aggressive predators like European Seabass and Bluefish.

LRF (Light Rock Fishing) Rods

Action & Specs: Ultra-lightweight setups. Usually 2.10m to 2.30m in length with incredibly flexible tips rated for ultra-low casting weights between 0.5g and 10g.

Primary Purpose: Focused on using micro silicone baits and tiny jigs near the shoreline. LRF allows anglers to target smaller predators (like Horse Mackerel and Rockfish) while maximizing the sensation of every single fish bite.

Surf Casting (Long Distance) Rods

Action & Specs: Heavy-duty and rigid construction. These powerful rods are usually 4.20m to 4.50m long, capable of casting heavy sinkers weighting from 100g up to 250g.

Primary Purpose: Specifically engineered to blast baits past the breaking surf into deep offshore channels or heavy sea currents. Ideal for targeting migratory species over long distances.

2. Artificial Lure Technologies

Hard Baits (Minnows & Plugs)

Features: Constructed from hard plastic or wood, featuring a front plastic lip (bib). The lip catches the water current, forcing the lure to swim erratically like a real panic-stricken fish.

Best Synergy: Highly effective when paired with Spin rods. Surface poppers or shallow diving minnows are lethal weapons for shallow-water Seabass hunting.

Soft Baits (Silicone Lures)

Features: Molded from flexible, soft plastics. Their paddle-tails or curly-tails create organic, low-frequency vibrations underwater. These are rigged onto weighted hooks known as "Jigheads".

Best Synergy: The absolute foundation of LRF fishing. Glow-in-the-dark or UV-reactive silicones dominate night fishing sessions for species like Horse Mackerel.

Metal Jigs

Features: Solid lead or metal alloy lures coated with highly reflective, aerodynamic paint. They sink rapidly and are retrieved with sharp, vertical rod jerks.

Best Synergy: Perfect for cutting through strong winds during long casts. Excellent for triggering reaction strikes from fast-swimming pelagic fish like Atlantic Bonito.
