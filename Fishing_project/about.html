Getting Started

Prepare calmly and fish the right way.

For beginners, the most important things are not expensive gear, but choosing the right spot, learning basic knots, staying safe and knowing local fishing limits.

01

Choose the spot

Check the target fish and weather for lakes, streams, reservoirs or shorelines.

02

Prepare the setup

Your rod, reel, line, hook and bait should match the species you are targeting.

03

Follow the rules

Legal sizes, closed seasons and daily limits can change by region.
