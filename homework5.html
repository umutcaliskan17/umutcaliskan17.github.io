Chosen Sites: CNN (cnn.com) and MSNBC (msnbc.com)

Both sites use consistent navigation menus at the top of every page. They repeat their brand colors in headings and icons.Example:CNN red, MSNBC blue.

Both sites use high contrast with dark text on a white background. Headlines are larger to stand out from body text.

Related stories are grouped in defined sections.

Both sites use a grid-based layout. The elements are aligned vertically and horizontally.
